01

Google Health & Fitbit Air

In May 2026 the Fitbit app became Google Health, alongside a new screenless tracker, the Fitbit Air. The job was to keep the trust Fitbit had built over years while defining what health and wellness look like to the Google Health brand.

The strategy: keep Fitbit's long-built trust at the center, and introduce Google's AI-powered health. Post-launch, I developed monthly social-first content strategies based on market trends, product evaluation and the cultural conversation.

What I owned
  • Positioning and brand personality
  • Customer segmentation and key selling features
  • Content strategy, launch calendar and creative briefs
  • Trusted voices, including Stephen Curry and Bryson DeChambeau

02

Fitbit Edge launch

Fitbit Edge brought a screen back to the lineup, sitting between the screenless Fitbit Air and the Pixel Watch.

The strategy: keep what people loved about Fitbit while adding a small display that brings stats to a glance, so the tracker keeps its simple, wear-and-forget feel. The launch also built on the Stephen Curry partnership, carrying it from the Fitbit Air into the Edge.

What I owned
  • Launch strategy on social, presented directly to Google
  • Positioning a screen without losing Fitbit's simple, low-key feel
  • Building the Stephen Curry partnership into a second launch

Fitbit Air Metal Mesh band

A stainless steel mesh band in Champagne Gold and Polished Silver, launched alongside the Fitbit Edge in October 2026.

The strategy: position a wearable as an accessory. The band pushed the brand toward high-end, editorial product, pulling Fitbit Air into luxury and speaking directly to Google Health's female audience.

What I owned

04

Google Workspace

Google Vids is Workspace's AI video app, with more features than any one post can carry. The work was deciding which features to lead with, for whom, and why.

Product troubleshooting was at the core: testing Vids hands-on showed which features were strongest. The live posts each match one feature to one audience: Slides into video pitches for professionals, everyday clips into promo videos for small businesses, and AI avatars and voiceovers for founders without a production budget.

What I owned
  • Product narrative strategy and audience segmentation
  • Product troubleshooting, plus market and competitor research, to choose which features to advertise
  • Aligning directly with Google on product and marketing strategy
